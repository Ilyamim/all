Our mission

We guide neighbors to steady ground.

Brightpath Volunteers is a community-led team of guides, listeners, and connectors. We equip families with resources, one-on-one support, and pathways to employment.

Mentorship

Weekly check-ins paired with goal setting so families stay focused on housing, education, and wellness priorities.

Immediate relief

Meal kits, rides to appointments, and childcare coverage remove barriers in moments that matter most.

Career readiness

Resume coaching, mock interviews, and employer connections help neighbors secure sustainable work.

Impact

Neighbors see real change in 90 days.

Brightpath volunteers partner with local case workers to ensure families reach stability goals quickly. We track outcomes across housing, employment, and wellness markers.

  • 87% of families remain housed after six months.
  • 760 job interviews scheduled last quarter.
  • 3,400 warm meals delivered since January.
